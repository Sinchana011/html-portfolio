My favourite web series

These are not ranked, just my random favourites

Big Bang Theory

A brilliant fusion of science and humor. My top pick for a sitcom with the most satisfying conclusion.

Brooklyn 99

It is a fun series with a lively cast, optimistic humor, and intriguing thrillers in every episode.

Queen's Gambit

It features brilliant cinematography and the background music that bring the story to life. It portrays Beth's struggles with drugs and alcohol addiction while striving to become the world's top chess player.
